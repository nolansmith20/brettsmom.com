01 — The record

She does not have a publicist. She has a kitchen table.

Brett's Mom keeps no office and no waiting room. Visits happen at the kitchen table, which has heard more career changes than most departments of human resources.

She became the most requested mom in the tri-county area the ordinary way: by feeding people, remembering their dogs' names, and offering advice nobody asked for and everybody needed. The dining chairs ran out sometime during the Mom of the Year streak. She is in year eleven. The trophy is a casserole dish she will not lend out.

Brett would like it known that he loves his mom, and that the science-fair story is, in his words, “context-dependent.” This page notes the objection and has already set an extra place.
